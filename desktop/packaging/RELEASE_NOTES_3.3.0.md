## What's new in 3.3.0

**An answer asked again keeps the one before it.** "Answer again" no longer throws the old answer away: each answer remembers its versions, and the arrows under it (‹ 1 / 2 ›) move between them. The one you leave on screen is the one the next turns build on.

**Edit a message you sent.** The pencil under your message opens it for editing; sending it starts a new branch from there. The old message and everything after it are kept, and the arrows under the message take you back and forth between branches.

**Ctrl+K: one box for everything.** Commands (a new chat, settings, models, the theme, the modes, the web), your chats by title, and — from three letters on — the words inside your messages, each with a line of context. Choosing a message opens its chat at that message.

**Slash commands.** Type `/` at the start of the message box for a short list: `/plan`, `/agents`, `/web`, `/new` (and their Persian names). Arrow keys and Enter, or a click.

**Search inside messages.** The sidebar's search now finds the words inside your messages too, not only chat titles.

**Run a page the model wrote.** HTML and SVG code gets a Run button: the page opens in a panel beside the chat, in a sandbox that cannot reach the studio or your files.

**PDF and Word files.** Drop a `.pdf` or `.docx` on the message box and its text is read on your computer and attached. The two readers are downloaded the first time they are needed, so this one needs the internet once.

**Read an answer aloud**, with your computer's own voices.

All conversations stay in the studio's folder on your computer, as before.
